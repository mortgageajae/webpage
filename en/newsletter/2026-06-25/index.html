BC's $3.2 Billion Housing Plan: Condo Purchases and Where the Market Is Headed

Published Thursday, June 25, 2026 · Rates, market figures and outlooks in this article reflect its publication date and may differ today. Ask us for the latest picture. Translated from the Korean original.

The federal government and the Province of BC recently officially announced a total of $3.2 billion in development support, along with a plan to purchase 2,200 unsold condos. This is an active government intervention aimed at clearing the problem inventory in today's weakened condo market and giving housing supply some breathing room.

Key Points

  • Major funding injection: Over the next 10 years, the federal government and BC will each contribute $1.6 billion on a matching basis to support new multi-unit housing projects.
  • Purchase of unsold condos: The government will directly buy about 2,200 vacant condos in major urban areas and convert them into Affordable Rental housing.
  • Lower costs: Development cost charges (DCCs) will be reduced by up to $40,000 per unit, lowering the barrier to building new homes.
  • Mixed market reactions: Critics call it a waste of taxpayer money (a bailout) for struggling developers, while others see it as an essential stabilization measure to prevent a chain-reaction collapse of the real estate and financial markets.

What It Means

This large-scale government intervention looks set to act as a strong line of defence against a sharp collapse of BC's condo market, which has recently been under heavy downward pressure. If it becomes clear that unsold inventory in the market can be absorbed first by public funds, existing homeowners' worries about falling property values and their psychological anxiety can also be eased to some degree. However, it is unrealistic to expect these artificial measures alone to bring about a dramatic rebound in the short term. A true market recovery requires macro rate trends and genuine end-user demand to come together, so if you are considering a home purchase or a mortgage plan, we recommend calmly watching the market as it works to find a bottom for the time being, and carefully reviewing your financing plans rather than making hasty decisions.
