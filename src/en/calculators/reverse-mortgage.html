Reverse Mortgage Estimate

If the amount is blank, the low end of the estimate is used. The 6.3% default is about where major lenders' 5-year fixed reverse mortgage rates sat in November 2026; higher-limit products for 70+ usually start around 7.4%.

How the estimate works

  • Age: the youngest owner's age sets the limit. It rises with age and usually levels off around 55% of home value by 75 to 80. Higher-limit products for 70+ go to 59 to 60%.
  • Location: lenders grade cities by size. Metro Vancouver, Greater Victoria and Kelowna get the highest limits, then mid-size cities, small towns, and the north and rural areas. Few lenders serve rural areas.
  • Home type: some lenders lend slightly less on townhouses and condos than on detached homes.
  • Home value: at least $250,000; above $2M the percentage gradually drops.
  • Couples: both owners must be 55+, and a large age gap pulls the result toward the younger owner.

Costs to expect

  • Lender set-up fee about $995 (taken from the advance)
  • Legal about $1,500: registration about $1,000 + independent legal advice (ILA, required) about $500
  • Appraisal extra
  • Prepayment charges are usually a few months' interest in the early years and shrink over time.

Before you decide

  • No payments are required, but interest keeps adding to the balance.
  • You must keep paying property tax and home insurance and maintain the home.
  • It is repaid when you sell, when the last owner dies, or when you move into care.
  • The bank does not get your home. Title stays in your name, and whatever is left after the balance is repaid goes to your heirs. They can also repay the balance and keep the home.
  • The money is a loan, not income, so it does not affect OAS or GIS.
